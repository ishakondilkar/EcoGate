IoT system architecture

01⌁

Sensor layer

Occupancy, temperature, humidity, daylight, air quality, energy and water inputs.

→
02◈

Processing layer

Simulated telemetry is evaluated by threshold-based decision rules.

→
03⚙

Decision engine

Lighting, HVAC, ventilation and water recommendations are calculated.

→
04▥

Dashboard layer

Operators view status, Eco Score, alerts and estimated energy performance.

Future implementation: this simulated architecture could be extended with ESP32/Arduino sensors, MQTT, a cloud database and real actuator integrations. These are not connected in this prototype.
